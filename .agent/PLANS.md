# ExecPlans

Use a concise, repository-local ExecPlan for documentation architecture, publication, supply-chain, security-boundary, migration, or multi-stage work. Store task plans in `.agent/plans/`.

Include: objective, existing behavior, proposed behavior, constraints, security considerations, milestones, validation, rollback considerations, decisions, and final outcome. For image references, record digests and public/private visibility decisions without including credentials.

Do not require a plan for a documentation correction, small bug fix, routine dependency bump, or other trivial change unless it meets one of the required criteria.
